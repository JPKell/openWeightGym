How scoring and recovery work

Role preference is multiplied by 0.5–1.1 using recent faults and optional feedback. Three consecutive faults exclude a candidate from that role. After five minutes, Run recovery sends one short request (up to 16 output tokens, 60 seconds) as you, at low priority; it may load the model or incur hosted cost. Success restores eligibility. Reset starts a fresh scoring window and retains history.
