Details

A role is a kind of work, such as chat or code. People and programs name the role where a model's name would go, and compute picks the model. Naming a model itself always works too. A role's models are ranked by your weights and any selected benchmarks. Equal scores keep the order in which the models were listed; 0 leaves a model out. These are preference weights, rather than percentages of requests. The name can say how much the pick matters:

ROLEThe highest preference after adding the role's loaded-model bonus to ready local residents. The default 25% bonus lets a loaded candidate with at least 80% of the strongest eligible score win.
ROLE:bestThe top of the list. It is waited for, and idle models are pushed off the cards to load it. For work that matters.
ROLE:availableThe highest in the list that is warm: loaded and ready. Nothing is loaded or pushed off unless nothing is warm.
ROLE:fastPrefer speed using queue/load estimates and the request's inferred prompt and maximum output budgets, divided by the role's fast reuse multiplier for ready local residents (×2 by default). This preview uses 400 output tokens. Unknown timing stays unavailable.

The :fast estimate uses 60 seconds to load and 10 output tokens/s when measurements are missing. A resident or warm model has no estimated load cost. Its reuse weighting is a preference, not a reduction in measured seconds. Prompt processing and queue wait are not included, so this is a comparison estimate, not a response-time guarantee. The previews below use your permissions and current model state; an actual request also checks its context, images, tools and sensitivity requirements.
