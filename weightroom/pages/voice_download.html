{% call card("Connect") %}
  1. Open the app's Settings tab and enter the desktop server address provided by your administrator.
  2. Copy your API key from My account, or import its existing key file.
  3. Choose Direct or Hermes, select your microphone and speakers, save, then press Start.

Your account needs Voice access, access to the selected model and permission to load it when needed. In Hermes mode, your speech is transcribed, the transcript is sent to Hermes, and Hermes's text reply is synthesized back to you.
