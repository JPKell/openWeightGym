{% call more('How scoring, scope and recovery work') %}

Role preference is multiplied by 0.5–1.1 using recent faults and optional feedback. Three consecutive faults exclude a candidate from that role. After a five-minute cooldown, a successful recovery check restores eligibility.

Counts and feedback follow log permissions; preference and recovery are shared routing state. Cancelled requests, interrupted benchmarks, restarts, admission refusals and unrecognized answers do not reduce preference. Historical fingerprints remain separate.

Issues only keeps candidates with an error, timeout or empty answer. A regression is a fault-rate increase of at least 20 percentage points versus the preceding sample.
