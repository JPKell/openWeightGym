Design rationale & suggested personal settings
{{ section_title('Three ways to organize the same settings') }}

01 · Settings desk — recommended. Stable category navigation and a focused editor fit the console’s established pattern. Context stays visible on wide screens; navigation becomes a wrapping button group on mobile.

02 · Personal overview. A calm account home makes security, preferences and activity easy to discover. Best for occasional users; reaching a form takes one additional step.

03 · Preferences notebook. All sections stay on one page as native disclosures with an index. Best for reviewing several preferences together; the page grows as sections open.
