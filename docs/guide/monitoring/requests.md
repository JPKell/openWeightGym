# Model requests

Find a request and inspect its timing, content and related activity.

## Do it

1. Open **Model requests** and narrow the retained list by its offered identity, model, node, time and outcome filters.
2. Open a request for **Diagnostics**, **Request & response**, **Related activity** or **Raw metadata**.
3. Use **Copy link** or **Export metadata ↓** to share authorized diagnostic context.

## Limits and troubleshooting

Captured text needs current owner/sensitivity permission and may have expired. Metadata export excludes retained prompts, replies and tool arguments. **Stop this request** appears only for authorized active work and needs confirmation. No-log work has no retained request record; a missing ending is distinct from a recorded failure.
