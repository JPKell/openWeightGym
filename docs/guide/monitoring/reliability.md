# Reliability

Inspect candidate health and the request evidence behind routing decisions.

## Do it

1. Open **Reliability** and choose the role/model and current or historical identity filters.
2. Select an attention item or candidate to inspect its outcome counts and retained evidence.
3. Follow issue request links before using an offered recovery or reset action.

## Limits and troubleshooting

Lifetime quality and model-specific request issues answer different questions. A historical fingerprint is not the current configuration. Missing retained requests cannot establish recovery. Recovery/reset controls retain their current authorization and confirmation requirements.
