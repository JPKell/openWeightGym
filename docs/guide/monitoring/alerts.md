# Alerts

Investigate operational episodes and distinguish acknowledgment from recovery.

## Do it

1. Open **Alerts**, choose an episode and inspect its timeline and investigation steps.
2. Select **Acknowledge** to record that it has been seen.
3. Follow the permitted investigation links. Use **Mark incident reviewed** for an incident requiring manual review.

## Options

The offered scopes distinguish open episodes, history, unacknowledged items, manual review and unknown recovery. Delivery status is shown alongside an episode when retained.

## Limits and troubleshooting

Acknowledgment does not resolve a condition. Missing telemetry can mean sleep, disconnection or a stopped service; it establishes neither failure nor recovery. Spending episodes use recorded costs and exclude unknown/in-flight costs. Configure notification policy under **Settings → Alerts**.
