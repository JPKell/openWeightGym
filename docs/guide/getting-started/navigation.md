# Find your way

Use the category menu to reach the pages your account is allowed to use.

## Do it

1. Choose **Interact**, **Models**, **Benchmark**, **Logs**, **System**, **Settings** or **Docs**. Empty categories and disabled features disappear.
2. Choose a page in that category's section menu. On a phone, open **Menu** to see both levels.
3. In **Docs**, enter a search phrase and optionally choose a section. **Clear** returns to browsing. Article contents and **Next** links help you read related topics.

**Find the guide for a page** on the Docs home maps available pages to their guides. Administrator references and private paragraphs are hidden from other accounts and their search results.

## Options

| Control | Use |
|---|---|
| `/` in Docs | Focus search when you are not typing in a field. |
| **My account** | Open the account menu by selecting your name. |
| **Chat options** / **Main menu** | Switch the smaller-screen chat drawer between conversation controls and navigation. |
