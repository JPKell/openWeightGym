# My account

Use the account menu to change your own preferences and security choices.

## Do it

1. Select your name, then **My account**. **Account** shows your group, sign-in expiry and active devices.
2. To change a password, fill **Current password**, **New password** and **Confirm new password**, then select **Change password**. This ends your other sign-ins.
3. Review **Your sign-ins**. **Sign out…** ends one other sign-in; **Sign out other devices…** ends the others after confirmation.

## Options

| Page | Use |
|---|---|
| **API keys** | Manage application credentials in the [API keys guide](/docs/web-app/api-keys). |
| **Appearance** | Preview **Color theme** and **Light or dark**, then **Save theme**. **Use default** resumes inheritance. Display/accessibility settings follow the account; theme choices belong to this account in this browser. |
| **Audio** | Save personal playback and audio workspace choices. |
| **Workspace** | Choose **Open after sign-in**, **Default chat model or role** and **Time zone**. Existing chats keep their selected model. For Civitai preview controls, see [Downloads](/docs/model-guides/downloads#civitai-previews). |
| **Alerts & privacy** | Choose personal notices and optional delivery; inspect **My delivery status**, **Recent notices** and **Data & access**. |
| **Logs** | Review your own account activity and usage. |

## Privacy and troubleshooting {#privacy}

Personal preferences do not change permissions or retained-record policies. **Export preferences** includes account and audio preferences; it excludes credentials, requests and conversation content. A default page or model can disappear when permission or platform policy changes. Your administrator controls password rules and authenticator requirements; contact them if you lose your authenticator.

Docs **Ask** keeps unanswered question text anonymously for documentation improvements, with a count and last-asked time. Administrators can dismiss entries. This list contains no account or answer; avoid private details in questions. Ask conversation history stays in the browser tab, while normal request usage/logging still applies.
