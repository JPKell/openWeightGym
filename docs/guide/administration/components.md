# Components

Inspect installed services and engines and use scoped lifecycle controls.

## Do it

1. Open **Components** and identify the control node or compute node you intend to inspect.
2. Review installed, missing and unknown service/engine evidence, including its recorded version-check time.
3. For an authorized lifecycle action, inspect its named service and node and confirm the existing control.

## Limits and troubleshooting

A reported version is evidence from its recorded check, not a new runtime qualification. Restart success alone does not establish the running source or live behavior. Preserve the established [installation and update workflow](/docs/operations/installation) and verify the affected service after an authorized change.
