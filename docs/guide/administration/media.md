# Media settings

Configure image, audio and Voice policies without confusing them with model editors.

## Do it

1. Choose **Settings → Image**, **Audio** or **Voice** for the service policy you intend to change.
2. Review the owning program/node, search the grouped fields and save the applicable form.
3. Check whether the change is live or pending a restart; test an authorized request after it becomes effective.

## Limits and troubleshooting

These policies do not install compatible model files. Use [Image models](/docs/model-guides/image-models) or [Audio models](/docs/model-guides/audio-models) for model setup. Voice profiles and recognition/synthesis choices need usable services and permitted models. [Shipped defaults and meanings](/docs/operations/settings-reference) are generated; current values remain in their owning settings page.
