# Users

Manage account identity, sign-in recovery, keys and individual access.

## Before you start

Use [account-management access](/docs/system/abilities#ability-manage-users); administrators cannot grant access beyond their own authority.

## Do it

1. Open **Users**, select **+ Add account**, choose the group and create the required person/app identity.
2. Copy the values under **Copy now: shown once** privately. The account's panels expose **Sign-in & group**, **API keys**, **Permissions** and **Limits & tools**.
3. Review changes and use the account's save action. **Rename** preserves identity; **Move** changes its group.

## Limits and troubleshooting

**Make a temporary password** and **Forget their authenticator** are explicit recovery actions. **Disable**, key removal and account removal have different scopes; read the confirmation. Recreating a removed name does not inherit the former account's private work. App sessions can do no more than the intersection of app and person access.
