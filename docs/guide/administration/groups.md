# Groups

Set shared abilities, model/tool access and inherited limits.

## Before you start

Use [group-management access](/docs/system/abilities#ability-manage-groups). The [Abilities reference](/docs/system/abilities) describes every available permission.

## Do it

1. Open **Groups**, select **+ Add group**, or choose an existing group.
2. Review **What members may do**, **Limits and signing in**, **Uploads and content limits**, and **Models and tools**.
3. Save the selected group. Use **Members** when moving accounts to a different group.

## Limits and troubleshooting

A new group allows nothing until configured. **Signed-in session** hours are above 0 and at most 8760 (one year); a group saved earlier with more ends its sessions after a year. Individual account overrides can further alter or narrow effective access. Blank content-limit overrides inherit live global policy; explicit values remain subject to catalogue validation. Deleting a group and moving its members are separate actions; review both before confirming. A group page left open in another tab cannot bring back a group that was removed: saving it says the group no longer exists and saves nothing. Nor can it undo a change saved since it opened: the save is refused, and the page shows what you typed over the latest settings to review and save again.
